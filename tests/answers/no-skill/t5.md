## What the email says

Your bank is doing a routine review, which regulations require. Their records show that your proof of address is out of date, so they want a new one.

## What you must do

1. **Send a valid proof of address by 15 December 2026.** Accepted documents are a utility bill or a bank statement issued within the last three months.
2. **Choose how to submit it:** upload it through the bank's secure portal, or bring it to a branch.

## What happens if you do not

From 15 December, outgoing transfers from your account would be limited to USD 500 per day until you send the document. Incoming payments and card purchases would not be affected. The email also says there is no fee.

## Check that the email is real first

Emails like this are a common way for scammers to pretend to be a bank, so be careful before you act:

- Do not click any link in the email. Go to the bank's website by typing the address yourself, or use the official app, and log in there.
- Call the number on the back of your card, as the email itself suggests, and ask whether they sent this request.
- Or just go to a branch with your document, which avoids the question.
- Never share your PIN or password. A real bank will not ask for them, and the email says the same.

The content of the email looks normal for a real review: it gives a deadline, it does not ask for a fee, and it does not ask for your password. But I cannot confirm from the text alone that it really came from your bank, so please check with them directly.
