## What the email means

Your bank must check its records of you from time to time. The law requires this. The bank says that your proof of address is old. So the bank needs a new one.

## What you must do

Send a valid proof of address before 15 December 2026. You can use one of these documents:

- a utility bill (a bill for electricity, water, or gas) from the last three months
- a bank statement from the last three months

You can send the document in two ways:

- Upload it in the secure portal of the bank.
- Take it to a branch.

## What happens if you do not send it

After 15 December 2026, the bank will limit your outgoing transfers to USD 500 each day. The limit stays until you send the document. Incoming payments and card purchases will not change. The email says that the process has no fee.

I cannot tell if this email is real. Some fake emails look like this. The email says that the bank will never ask for your PIN or password, and that is correct advice. So take these steps:

1. Do not click a link in the email, if it has one.
2. Open the website or the app of your bank yourself. You can also call the number on the back of your card.
3. Ask the bank if it really needs a proof of address from you.
4. Send the document only in the portal of the bank or at a branch.

If the bank says that the email is real, the steps above are all you need.
